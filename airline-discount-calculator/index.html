The QFC calculator is designed for travel agents to review airline discount and PSF calculations. Select an airline, booking type, basic fare, taxes, segment information and passenger counts.

The calculator supports separate adult, child and infant passenger quantities and displays per-person amounts, subtotals and a grand total.
